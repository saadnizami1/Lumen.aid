# Lumen.aid

**Not everyone experiences light the same way.**

Lumen.aid is a youth led initiative in Lahore, Pakistan, empowering visually impaired
communities. We are fifteen students, most of us in our A Levels, and we started this in
2024 because the barriers blind people face here are not mostly medical. They are about
access: tools, schools, streets, work.

## The problem we work on

- An estimated 1.8 million people in Pakistan are blind.
- Around 20 million live with some form of visual impairment.
- Around three quarters of it is preventable or treatable.

For those whose sight is not coming back, what can change is how much of the world they can
navigate on their own. That is where we focus.

## What we do

**Fundraisers.** Community events like café screenings and campus drives, where everything
raised goes directly to the blind community. So far we have raised PKR 150,000+ across our
events.

**Assistive support.** White canes, screen reader setup and everyday tools, funded by what
we raise and given free to the people who need them. We have also helped fund cataract
surgery for patients who could not afford it.

**Partnerships.** We work alongside established organisations like LRBT (Layton Rahmatulla
Benevolent Trust), which provides free eye care across Pakistan, and connect people to
services that already exist.

**Awareness.** Interviews and social content that put blind voices first, on campus and
online, plus plain language articles on eye health.

## Offline Dekho, our app

[Offline Dekho](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.saadnizami.offlinedekho&hl=en)
is our free Android app for people who are permanently blind. Say "idhar udhar dekhna" and
the app describes what the camera sees, out loud. The computer vision runs entirely on the
phone: no internet needed, no data costs, and every image stays private on the device.

## About this website

This site is built to be genuinely usable by the community we serve: it works with screen
readers and keyboards, honours reduced motion settings (with a visible toggle on the
landing page), and ships no trackers and no cookies. Technical notes for contributors are
in [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md).
